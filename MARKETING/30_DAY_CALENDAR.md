# 🗓️ 30-DAY LAUNCH CALENDAR (Full-Time)

Your daily marching orders. Each day = ~3–5 focused hours. All the content you need is in `CONTENT_PACK.md`. Check the box when done.

> **Daily baseline once you're live (from Day 4):** 3 Pinterest pins + 1 LinkedIn post + 1 TikTok/Reel + engage 30 min. That's the engine. Everything else is on top.

---

## WEEK 1 — Get Live & Start the Engine

**Day 1 — Foundation**
- [ ] Sign up for **Dodo Payments**, start identity verification.
- [ ] Open your 6 product files; take 3–4 screenshots for listing images.
- [ ] Set up a free **Canva** account (you'll make Pinterest pins here).

**Day 2 — Store Live**
- [ ] Connect **Payoneer** as payout in Dodo.
- [ ] Create the product listing (paste from `LISTING_KIT.md`), upload the zip of files 00–05 + cover + screenshots.
- [ ] Set intro price **$7**. **Publish.** Copy your store link.
- [ ] Paste store link into the lead magnet (`LEAD_MAGNET_10_Free_Prompts.pdf`) where marked.

**Day 3 — Platforms Setup**
- [ ] Create/optimize accounts: **Pinterest (business), TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Reddit.**
- [ ] Use the **LinkedIn checklist** on your OWN profile (you're now a career creator).
- [ ] Make 5 Pinterest pins in Canva (use titles 1–5 from Content Pack).

**Day 4 — First Content Day**
- [ ] Post Pinterest pins 1–3, LinkedIn Post 1, TikTok Hook 1.
- [ ] Join 5 Facebook job-seeker groups + 3 relevant subreddits (just join, observe rules).

**Day 5 — Value Push**
- [ ] 3 Pinterest pins, LinkedIn Post 2, 1 Reel (Script 1).
- [ ] Post 1 genuine value comment on Reddit (no link).
- [ ] Answer 1 Quora question.

**Day 6 — Local Market**
- [ ] Set up local sales flow: Instagram + WhatsApp, price in PKR, Google Drive delivery link ready.
- [ ] Post 3 Pinterest pins, LinkedIn Post 3 (soft sell), 1 Reel.

**Day 7 — Review**
- [ ] Check: which posts got the most views/clicks? Note it.
- [ ] 3 Pinterest pins, Instagram caption 1. Rest a bit. 🎯 **Goal: 1–3 sales this week.**

## WEEK 3 — Scale What Works

- [ ] **Double down** on your best channel (likely Pinterest). Make 5 pins/day there.
- [ ] **Repurpose:** turn each of your 100 prompts into a micro-post (use AI — see playbook).
- [ ] **Email capture:** set up a free email tool (MailerLite free tier) → give the lead magnet in exchange for email.
- [ ] Keep LinkedIn at 1 post/day, 1 Reel/day.
- [ ] Engage 30 min/day: comment on bigger creators' posts in your niche.
- [ ] **Day 21 goal:** email list started, top channel identified, **5–15 total sales.**

## WEEK 4 — Compound & Expand

- [ ] Keep the daily engine running. **Day 30 goal: 10–40 total sales + a growing list + a content library that keeps pulling traffic while you sleep.**
